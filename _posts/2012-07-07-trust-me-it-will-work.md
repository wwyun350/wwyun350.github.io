Introduction, Background, and Problem Statement
The team noted that at Dalian Maritime University, due to its large campus area and the absence of campus buses, getting from point A to Point B on foot is very difficult, especially when one is carrying bulky luggage.
For example, at the beginning of a semester, when a large number of students return to campus, they carry a large number of packages that are difficult to transport to the corresponding dormitories at one time by themselves, they often need the help of another two or more individuals to complete the work. This situation is especially obvious in September every year, when the new students are coming to the school. 
In this case, a transport vehicle, designed for short and medium range transportation, can solve this problem at low cost yet highly effective. By referring to the vehicles used in large warehouses, the team decided to design and manufacture a transporter with a lifting platform to solve the need of large package transportation from building to building in our campus.

According to Optimization of Personnel Flow Management Method in Colleges and Universities Based on BIM + MassMotion [1], a study by WANG Huiqian (2024), the number of people gathered at the campus express station on the start/end of a semester was significantly greater than that on any other times of a year, over 70% of university students reported difficulties in transporting large items across campus, particularly during peak periods such as the start of the semester. This aligns with our observations at DMU. Another source, Research on an Intelligent disinfection and sterilization Transport Vehicle for Campus Epidemic Management [2] by LV Airong (2023), conducted that universities with larger campus areas, face unique challenges related to student transport efficiency. The same study suggests that introducing lightweight transport vehicles can reduce travel time by up to 40% for short distances, enhancing overall campus accessibility.

As stated in the proposal pitch, the project aims to design a compact transporter within the dimensions of 2m (L) x 1m (W) x 1.5m (H) and a budget of 10,000 RMB, capable of carrying up to 500 kg (600 kg safety margin). It will operate on a 24V DC power supply with a minimum battery life of 8 hours and a maximum charging time of 4 hours, using lithium-ion batteries for efficiency. The design incorporates communication protocols like RS-232, CAN bus, or Ethernet for system integration. A geared DC motor will enable a transport speed of at least 1 meter/second, improving transport efficiency by reducing manual transport time by 30%. The focus on structural integrity, lightweight materials, and user-friendly controls ensures practical functionality for campus logistics.

Technical Advisor: Dr. Yingze Jin, an expert in tribology and dynamics of rotating machinery, will oversee the project and provide guidance throughout the design process.
Team members: Yongyan Zhao, Chenyang Pan, Guangyun Wu, a team of students majoring in mechanical engineering, will participate in the design, prototyping, and testing phases.

The project is set to commence on September 1, 2024, with an expected completion date of May 1, 2025. So far, we have done initial work such as a preliminary feasibility study and analyzing potential design concepts. Prior to this report, we have also conducted a brainstorming session to outline the main features and functionalities of the transport vehicle, setting the foundation for detailed design work moving forward.
