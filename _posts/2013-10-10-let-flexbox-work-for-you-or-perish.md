1. Prototype Testing
Objective: Validate the physical working prototype against performance metrics.
Procedures: 
1.   Conduct load tests to assess maximum weight capacity (up to 500 kg) using calibrated weights.
2.   Measure speed and maneuverability through timed trials over a designated course, noting acceleration, turning radius, and braking distance.
3.   Assess battery life through controlled usage scenarios, logging operational hours under varying loads and conditions.
Measurements: Weight capacity (kg), speed (m/s), operational time (hours).

2. Simulation Verification
Objective: Validate the conceptual design through simulation results.
Procedures:
1.Utilize simulation software to model structural integrity and performance under different load conditions.
2.Execute simulations for stability analysis, focusing on weight distribution and center of gravity during movement and loading.
3.Compare predicted performance metrics (e.g., power consumption, load handling) with actual prototype testing results.
Measurements: Stress distribution (N/m²), power consumption (W), stability metrics (degrees).

3. Feasibility Evaluation
Objective: Validate feasibility study findings against practical scenarios.
Procedures:
1.Test the transporter in various environmental conditions (e.g., wet surfaces, inclines) to evaluate operational effectiveness and safety.
2.Gather feedback from potential users (e.g., campus staff) during trial runs to assess usability and functionality.
3.Review any failure points identified during testing to refine the design.
Measurements: Performance under adverse conditions (success/failure rate), user satisfaction (surveys).

4. Documentation Check
Objective: Ensure all schematics and assembly instructions are accurate and comprehensive.
Procedures:
1.Conduct a review of schematics by a qualified engineer to ensure all components are correctly represented.
2.Assemble a prototype using the provided documentation to verify clarity and completeness of instructions.
Measurements: Assembly time (hours), error rate during assembly (number of issues).
